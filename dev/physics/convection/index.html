Convection

A depth-averaged model has no vertical structure to overturn. Where the layer becomes denser than the ambient water beneath it, $\delta\rho < 0$, it is convectively unstable and would in reality mix away; the model has to say what happens instead. The treatment is selected with Params(; convection = ...):

schemeactionwhere
ResetToAmbientConvection$T, S$ reset to ambient at once, with $S$ lowered just enough for a contrast of d_rho_minice-covered cells
ClampDensityConvection$\delta\rho$ floored at d_rho_min, $T$ and $S$ left alonethe whole active domain
RelaxToAmbientConvection (default, 1 h)$T, S$ relaxed toward ambient over convection_timeice-covered cells

The thresholds d_rho_min are dimensional contrasts $\rho_a - \rho$ in kg m⁻³, divided by Params.rho0_seawater to compare with the reduced contrast $\delta\rho$: the default 0.005 kg m⁻³ is $\delta\rho \approx 4.9\times10^{-6}$, the buoyancy floor of LADDIE v2. ClampDensityConvection and ResetToAmbientConvection act wherever $\delta\rho$ is below that threshold, so on marginally stable cells as well as unstable ones. Only the unstable cells, $\delta\rho < 0$, are flagged in model.convective_instability. The salinity offset of the reset makes the contrast exactly the threshold under any equation of state, because $T = T_a$ removes the thermal term.

RelaxToAmbientConvection acts through the tracer equations, as a sink

\[-(q^{p} - q_a)\,\mathrm{conv2}, \qquad \mathrm{conv2} = \frac{[\delta\rho < 0]\,H}{\mathrm{convection\_time}},\]

evaluated at the past leapfrog level (see the equation reference). Its default convection_time of one hour is the order of the time an unstable layer takes to overturn, and gave good pan-Antarctic runs. The two others act on the fields directly, once at the start of each step, before the melt and entrainment are computed, and again after the Robert–Asselin filter.

Convection in gaps

The ambient profile in an ice-free gap is sampled at the sea surface, the coldest and freshest water in the column, so the layer there reads as convectively unstable almost unconditionally. ResetToAmbientConvection and RelaxToAmbientConvection are therefore restricted to ice-covered cells: resetting a gap cell towards surface ambient would erase the temperature and salinity anomaly the layer is carrying and quietly rebuild the meltwater sink that ConnectedGapsBC exists to remove. ClampDensityConvection is not restricted — a buoyancy floor keeps the layer lighter than ambient without overwriting its tracers, and it is the one convection treatment that LADDIE v2 also applies over its whole active domain.

LeakyGapsBC brings its own convection to gap cells, independent of the scheme chosen here: where the layer turns denser than the water beneath it, it relaxes towards that water and detrains, as described in Leaky gaps.

API

Laddie.AbstractConvection — Type

Abstract supertype for the treatment of convective instability: cells where the layer is denser than the ambient water beneath it, i.e. where the reduced density contrast δρ falls below zero. Pass a concrete instance as Params(; convection = ...): RelaxToAmbientConvection (the default), ResetToAmbientConvection or ClampDensityConvection.

No LADDIE paper describes a convection closure: ResetToAmbientConvection and RelaxToAmbientConvection follow the LADDIE v1 code (Lambert et al. 2023), ClampDensityConvection the buoyancy floor of the LADDIE v2 code (Lambert et al. 2026).

source
Laddie.ResetToAmbientConvection — Type
struct ResetToAmbientConvection{FT} <: AbstractConvection

Handle convective instability by instantly resetting T and S to their ambient values wherever the contrast falls below d_rho_min / Params.rho0_seawater, with S lowered by d_rho_min / (rho0_seawater β), with β the haline coefficient of the equation of state, so that the reset cell has exactly that contrast.

Select via Params(; convection = ResetToAmbientConvection(0.005)).

Fields

  • d_rho_min::Any = 0.005: threshold density contrast ρ_a − ρ that triggers the reset (kg m⁻³, default 0.005); divided by Params.rho0_seawater to compare with δρ
source
Laddie.ClampDensityConvection — Type
struct ClampDensityConvection{FT} <: AbstractConvection

Handle convective instability (δρ < 0) by clamping the density contrast to a minimum positive value, d_rho_min / Params.rho0_seawater, so the plume remains lighter than ambient. T and S are left unchanged. This is the buoyancy floor of LADDIE v2, max(drho_amb, buoyancy_min/rho_sw) with buoyancy_min = 5e-3.

Select via Params(; convection = ClampDensityConvection(0.005)).

Fields

  • d_rho_min::Any = 0.005: minimum density contrast ρ_a − ρ (kg m⁻³, default 0.005); divided by Params.rho0_seawater to compare with δρ
source
Laddie.RelaxToAmbientConvection — Type
struct RelaxToAmbientConvection{FT} <: AbstractConvection

Handle convective instability by relaxing T and S of unstable cells toward ambient values over a prescribed timescale (applied implicitly in the tracer time step as the sink −(q − q_a) conv2, conv2 = [δρ < 0] H/convection_time. The default; a continuous alternative to ResetToAmbientConvection.

Select via Params(; convection = RelaxToAmbientConvection(3600.0)) (the default).

Fields

  • convection_time::Any = 3600.0: relaxation timescale (s, default 3600)
