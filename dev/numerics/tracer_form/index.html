Tracer form

The temperature and salinity budgets are in flux form, $\partial(Hq)/\partial t = -\nabla\cdot(H\mathbf{u}q) + \dots$, and can be stepped in two forms, Params(; tracer_form = ...):

  • FluxTracerForm, the default, steps the contents $HT$ and $HS$ and divides by the new thickness, as LADDIE v2 does:

    \[(Hq)^{+} = H^{-} q^{-} + 2\Delta t\,\mathrm{rhs}, \qquad q^{+} = \frac{(Hq)^{+}}{H^{+}}.\]

  • AdvectiveTracerForm steps $T$ and $S$ themselves, with the product-rule term $-q\,\partial_t H$ taken from the thickness step just made, as LADDIE v1 does:

    \[q^{+} = q^{-} + 2\Delta t\,\frac{\mathrm{rhs} - q\,\partial_t H}{H^{n}},\]

    with rhs the same right-hand side. Select it explicitly to reproduce LADDIE v1; the verification against the v1 code and the Crosson–Dotson example do.

The flux form is the default because of how the two differ. They differ when the discrete $\partial_t H$ does not match the volume sources of the same step, which happens wherever the thickness step is clamped or restarted. In the advective form that mismatch is divided by $H$, and in thin layers it can drive $q$ far outside the range of the waters that make it up. In the flux form the sources enter the tracer only in proportion to the volume they bring, so the mismatch cannot be amplified. The advective form also carries a larger time-step error: on a 2-day warm ISOMIP+ run, cutting the step from 210 s to 52.5 s moves the mean melt by 7.3 % under the advective form and by 0.4 % under the flux form.

Where the clamp after the thickness step moves $H^{+}$ off the stepped value $H^{*}$, the flux form gives the moved water its matching tracer, as the preventive bounds of LADDIE v2 do: volume cut at the upper bound leaves at the layer's own $q$, volume added by the H_min floor comes in at the ambient $q_a$,

\[q^{+} = \frac{(Hq)^{+} + \max(H^{+} - H^{*}, 0)\,q_a}{\max(H^{+}, H^{*})}.\]

The clamp binds mostly in the bootstrap step, where the minimum-thickness entrainment and the cap detrainment, sized for a $2\Delta t$ step, do only half their work.

API

Laddie.AbstractTracerForm — Type
abstract type AbstractTracerForm

Abstract supertype for the form in which the temperature and salinity equations are stepped. Pass a concrete instance as Params(; tracer_form = ...): FluxTracerForm (the default) or AdvectiveTracerForm.

Both integrate the same flux-form budget ∂(Hq)/∂t = -∇·(H u q) + … and differ only in what they step: the tracer q itself, or its content H q.

source
Laddie.AdvectiveTracerForm — Type
struct AdvectiveTracerForm <: AbstractTracerForm

Step the tracer itself, as LADDIE v1 does: the budget is expanded by the product rule into H ∂q/∂t = … - q ∂H/∂t, with ∂H/∂t taken from the thickness step just made, and

q⁺ = q⁻ + 2Δt · rhs / Hⁿ.

The - q ∂H/∂t term cancels the volume sources only as far as the discrete ∂H/∂t matches them in the same step. It does not wherever the thickness step is clamped or restarted, and the mismatch is then divided by H: in thin layers this can drive q far outside the range of the waters that make it up.

Select it explicitly, Params(; tracer_form = AdvectiveTracerForm()), to reproduce LADDIE v1, as its verification test does.

source
Laddie.FluxTracerForm — Type
struct FluxTracerForm <: AbstractTracerForm

Step the tracer content (the default), as LADDIE v2 does (laddie_tracers.f90), and recover the tracer by dividing by the new thickness:

(Hq)⁺ = H⁻ q⁻ + 2Δt · rhs,     q⁺ = (Hq)⁺ / H⁺,

where rhs is the advective tracer rhs without its - q ∂H/∂t term. The sources then enter the tracer only in proportion to the volume they bring, so a mismatch between the thickness and tracer budgets cannot be amplified by a thin layer.

Where the clamp after the thickness step moves H⁺ off the stepped value H*, the moved water carries its matching tracer, as LADDIE v2's preventive bounds do: volume cut at the upper bound leaves at the layer's own q, volume added by the H_min floor comes in at the ambient q_a,

q⁺ = ((Hq)⁺ + max(H⁺ - H*, 0) q_a) / max(H⁺, H*).

The clamp binds mostly in the bootstrap step, where ent2 and the cap detrainment, sized for a 2Δt step, do only half their work.

Select via Params(; tracer_form = FluxTracerForm()) (the default).
