Open-ocean boundary

At the ice front, and at the edges of gaps under SinkGapsBC, the layer meets the open ocean. Layer properties leave freely; what enters is set by BoundaryConditions(; open_ocean = ...):

  • ZeroGradientOpenOceanBC, LADDIE v1 and v2: all fields are extrapolated with zero gradient, so inflow from the ambient ocean carries the properties of the cell it enters.
  • NoInflowOpenOceanBC: where the flow enters through an ice-front face, the thickness and tracer fluxes carry the (zero) value stored in the open-ocean cell, so no volume, heat or salt is advected in. The velocities themselves are not clipped, and the momentum advection is unaffected.
  • OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC: a one-way valve. After each momentum step the new face-normal velocity at every ice-front face is clipped to point out of the cavity, so the layer can leave but never enter; the fluxes carry nothing in, as under NoInflowOpenOceanBC, while an inward start fades from the filtered level. Under the other two, an inflow face either recirculates the front cell's own water (thickening and cooling the front row) or keeps an inward velocity that carries nothing (thinning it). Under the valve the layer is fed only by entrainment and melt. Ambient water enters a cavity mostly below the plume; where it enters at the ice base, the layer sees its temperature and salinity through entrainment, but not its momentum.

The valve is the default, with ZeroGradientFrontPressure. On a warm ISOMIP+ cavity at 4 km over 20 days, against zero-gradient inflow with V1FrontPressure:

The pressure gradient across the same faces is a separate choice; see Ice-front pressure gradient.

API

Laddie.AbstractOpenOceanBC — Type

Abstract supertype for the open-ocean boundary condition at the ice front. Pass a concrete instance as BoundaryConditions(; open_ocean = ...): OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC (the default), ZeroGradientOpenOceanBC or NoInflowOpenOceanBC.

source
Laddie.ZeroGradientOpenOceanBC — Type
struct ZeroGradientOpenOceanBC <: AbstractOpenOceanBC

Open-boundary condition at the ice front: zero-gradient extrapolation of all fields, with inflow from the ambient ocean permitted.

LADDIE v1 (boundop = 1) and v2 use this condition; the reproduction aliases pin it.

Select via BoundaryConditions(; open_ocean = ZeroGradientOpenOceanBC()).

source
Laddie.NoInflowOpenOceanBC — Type
struct NoInflowOpenOceanBC <: AbstractOpenOceanBC

Open-boundary condition at the ice front: no inflow of layer properties. Where the flow enters through an ice-front face, the thickness and tracer fluxes carry the (zero) value of the open-ocean cell, so no volume, heat or salt is advected in. The velocities are not clipped.

Select via BoundaryConditions(; open_ocean = NoInflowOpenOceanBC()).

source
Laddie.OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC — Type
struct OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC <: AbstractOpenOceanBC

Ice-front boundary as a one-way valve: the layer leaves through the front but never enters. After each momentum step the new face-normal velocity at every one-sided face (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC) is clipped to point out of the cavity (clip_front_inflow!), smoothly under SmoothKinks; the fluxes carry nothing in, as under NoInflowOpenOceanBC.

The default, with ZeroGradientFrontPressure; the comparison with the other conditions is on the Open-ocean boundary page.

Select via BoundaryConditions(; open_ocean = OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC()) (the default).
