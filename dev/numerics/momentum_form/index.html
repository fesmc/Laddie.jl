Momentum form and closure level

The momentum budget is in flux form, $\partial(HU)/\partial t = -\nabla\cdot(HU\mathbf{u}) + \dots$, and can be stepped in two forms, Params(; momentum_form = ...), the momentum counterpart of the tracer form:

  • AdvectiveMomentumForm, the default, steps $U$ itself with the product-rule term $-U\,\partial_t H$ and every term at the present level, as LADDIE v1 does:

    \[U^{+} = U^{-} + 2\Delta t\,\frac{\mathrm{rhs} - U\,\partial_t H - s\,U}{\bar H^{n}}.\]

  • FluxMomentumForm steps the transport $\bar H U$ and divides by the new face thickness, as LADDIE v2 does, with the terms that remove momentum at the layer's own $U$ taken at the new level:

    \[U^{+} = \frac{\bar H^{-} U^{-} + 2\Delta t\,\mathrm{rhs}}{\bar H^{+} + 2\Delta t\, s}, \qquad s = d + d_\mathrm{cap} + f + C_d\,|\mathbf{u}|,\]

    with $d$ the detrainment, $d_\mathrm{cap}$ the cap detrainment and $f$ the frazil formation, averaged onto the face.

Separately, Params(; closure_level = ...) sets the time level the closures read: the friction velocity $u_\star$ (hence melt and entrainment), the thickness in the entrainment rate and the drag speed $|\mathbf{u}|$, either PresentClosures (the default) or PastClosures.

Equations

The continuous system is the same under both forms. Melt $\dot m$ and entrainment $\dot e$ bring in water at rest, so they carry no momentum and only dilute $U$ through $H$. Detrainment $\dot d$, cap detrainment $\dot d_\mathrm{cap}$ and frazil formation $\dot f$ remove water at the layer's own velocity. Together with the quadratic drag they form one rate $s$ acting on $U$:

\[\begin{aligned} &\frac{\partial H}{\partial t} + \nabla\cdot(H\mathbf{u}) = \dot m + \dot e - \dot d - \dot d_\mathrm{cap} - \dot f, \\ &\frac{\partial (HU)}{\partial t} + \nabla\cdot(H\mathbf{u}U) - fHV = P_x + \nabla\cdot(A_h H\nabla U) - s\,U, \\ &\frac{\partial (HV)}{\partial t} + \nabla\cdot(H\mathbf{u}V) + fHU = P_y + \nabla\cdot(A_h H\nabla V) - s\,V, \\ &s = \dot d + \dot d_\mathrm{cap} + \dot f + C_d\,|\mathbf{u}|, \end{aligned}\]

with the pressure gradient $P_x = -\frac{gH^2}{2\rho_0}\partial_x\Delta\rho_a + g_a' H\,\partial_x(H_{ib} - H)$. In the code, $\dot e - \dot d$ is nentr.

In the leapfrog, $n-1$ is the filtered past level. Let $R^n$ collect the advection, Coriolis, pressure and viscosity terms at the present level, and let $\bar H$ be the face average of $H$. The advective form applies the product rule and takes every term at level $n$:

\[U^{n+1} = U^{n-1} + \frac{2\Delta t}{\bar H^{n}} \Big[R^{n} - U^{n}\,\overline{\partial_t H}^{\,n} - s^{n}\,U^{n}\Big], \qquad \partial_t H^{\,n} = \frac{H^{n+1} - H^{n-1}}{2\Delta t}.\]

The flux form steps the transport, and the sinks act on $U^{n+1}$:

\[\bar H_\ast^{\,n+1}\,U^{n+1} = \bar H^{\,n-1}\,U^{n-1} + 2\Delta t\,\big[R^{n} - s\,U^{n+1}\big] \quad\Longrightarrow\quad U^{n+1} = \frac{\bar H^{\,n-1}\,U^{n-1} + 2\Delta t\,R^{n}}{\bar H_\ast^{\,n+1} + 2\Delta t\,s},\]

where $\bar H_\ast^{\,n+1}$ is the face average of $\max(H^{n+1}, H^\ast)$ and $H^\ast$ is the thickness before the clamp. Under UpstreamMomentumAdvection, the advection in $R^n$ contains the face's own outflow $-o\,U^n$. It is moved to the new level as well:

\[U^{n+1} = \frac{\bar H^{\,n-1}\,U^{n-1} + 2\Delta t\,\big(R^{n} + o\,U^{n}\big)} {\bar H_\ast^{\,n+1} + 2\Delta t\,(s + o)}.\]

Since $R^n + o\,U^n$ holds only the inflow, $U^{n+1}$ is a weighted mean of $U^{n-1}$ and the momentum coming in. Under PastClosures, $s$ and the $u_\star$ in $\dot m$ and $\dot e$ are evaluated at $n-1$.

The advective form takes $\dot d$ at the tracer point $(i, j)$ and does not average it onto the face, as LADDIE v1 does (-detr * U in intU/intV). The term is therefore shifted half a cell: a $U$ face sees only the detrainment of the cell to its west, and a $V$ face only that of the cell to its south. It is kept that way so that the default stays bit-identical to LADDIE v1. The flux form averages $\dot d$ onto the face, as it does $\dot d_\mathrm{cap}$ and $\dot f$ under both forms.

Why

A term that damps the state it is computed from is unstable in the leapfrog at the centred level once its rate $\lambda$ is large: with the Robert–Asselin filter at $\nu = 0.8$, the limit is $\lambda\Delta t \approx 0.57$, and beyond it the computational mode flips the state every step. Entrainment of water at rest dilutes $U$ at the rate $e/H$, and since Gaspar's rate grows with $u_\star^3 \propto |U|^3$ the linearised rate is $4e/H$. In thin, strongly entraining layers it exceeds the limit ($\lambda\Delta t \approx 0.6$ in the wall corners of the 2 km pan-Antarctic domain at $\Delta t = 413$ s); quadratic drag, at $2C_d|\mathbf{u}|/H$, follows at $H \approx 1$ m. The primal stays bounded, held by the floors and the speed cap, but the alternation biases the mean entrainment and melt of the cell and its tangent grows without bound.

The two switches remove the two parts of that rate. Under FluxMomentumForm the dilution acts through $\bar H^{+}$, the factor $\bar H^{-}/\bar H^{+} \le 1$, and drag and detrainment through the denominator: unconditionally stable for a frozen rate. Under PastClosures the dependence of the rates on the state, $e(u_\star, H)$ and $|\mathbf{u}|$, comes from the lagged level, as LADDIE v1 lags the diffusion and the convective relaxation. For the scalar model $y' = -\lambda y$ with the rate split as in $e(U)\,U$, the stability limit moves from $\lambda\Delta t = 0.57$ to $1.15$ under the flux form alone, and beyond $20$ with both.

The same holds for advection: under UpstreamMomentumAdvection the momentum that leaves a face's control volume, $M_\mathrm{out}\,U$, is divided by $\bar H$, and a thin face next to thick donors loses more than it holds in one step. The flux form takes that outflow at the new level too, as FluxTracerForm does for the tracers, so $U^{+}$ is a weighted mean of $U^{-}$ and the momentum coming in.

In the 2 km pan-Antarctic domain at $\Delta t = 413$ s, the flux form removes the alternation at both wall corners where it occurred, one from each mechanism, and the cells settle where a run at $\Delta t/2$ puts them.

Both defaults reproduce the earlier integration bit for bit. On warm ISOMIP+ all four combinations give the same mean melt to 0.1 %.

API

Laddie.AbstractMomentumForm — Type
abstract type AbstractMomentumForm

Abstract supertype for the form in which the momentum equations are stepped. Pass a concrete instance as Params(; momentum_form = ...): AdvectiveMomentumForm (the default) or FluxMomentumForm.

Both integrate the same budget ∂(HU)/∂t = -∇·(H U u) + …, the momentum counterpart of AbstractTracerForm, and differ in what they step: the velocity U itself, or the transport H U.

source
Laddie.AdvectiveMomentumForm — Type
struct AdvectiveMomentumForm <: AbstractMomentumForm

Step the velocity itself, as LADDIE v1 does (the default): the budget is expanded by the product rule into H ∂U/∂t = … - U ∂H/∂t, with all terms at the present level, and

U⁺ = U⁻ + 2Δt · rhs / H̄ⁿ.

Water entrained at rest dilutes the momentum through - U ∂H/∂t, a damping at the rate e/H evaluated at the centred level. The leapfrog with the Robert–Asselin filter (ν = 0.8) holds such a term only up to a rate of about 0.57/Δt, and Gaspar's rate grows with |U|³, which makes the linearised rate 4e/H: thin, fast, strongly entraining layers exceed it, and the computational mode then flips U every step. FluxMomentumForm takes these terms at the new level.

source
Laddie.FluxMomentumForm — Type
struct FluxMomentumForm <: AbstractMomentumForm

Step the transport H̄U and recover the velocity by dividing by the new face thickness, as LADDIE v2 does (laddie_velocity.f90):

U⁺ = (H̄⁻ U⁻ + 2Δt · rhs) / (H̄⁺ + 2Δt · s),     s = d + d_cap + f + C_d |u| + o,

where rhs is the advective rhs without its - U ∂H/∂t term and without the terms that remove momentum at the layer's own U: the detrainment d, the cap detrainment d_cap, the frazil formation f (all averaged onto the face), the quadratic drag and, under UpstreamMomentumAdvection, the outflow o of the control volume's own velocity through its faces. Those enter as the rate s at the new level, with the drag speed |u| at the level of AbstractClosureLevel. The outflow is the momentum counterpart of the tracer outflow of FluxTracerForm: explicit, a thin face next to thick donors loses more momentum per step than it holds, and the leapfrog flips its sign every step.

Water entrained at rest then dilutes U through H̄⁺ alone, by the factor H̄⁻/H̄⁺ ≤ 1 whatever the rate, and water leaving at U leaves it unchanged. Under upstream advection U⁺ is a weighted mean of U⁻ and the momentum coming in. All of these are unconditionally stable, where the advective form becomes unstable at a damping rate of about 0.57/Δt (see AdvectiveMomentumForm).

Where the clamp after the thickness step moves H⁺ off the stepped value H*, the moved water carries its matching momentum, as in FluxTracerForm: volume cut at the upper bound leaves at U, volume added by the H_min floor comes in at rest, so the denominator takes the face average of max(H⁺, H*).

Select via Params(; momentum_form = FluxMomentumForm()).

Laddie.AbstractClosureLevel — Type
abstract type AbstractClosureLevel

Abstract supertype for the time level the closures see: the friction velocity u★ (hence the melt and entrainment rates), the thickness in the entrainment rate, and the speed in the quadratic drag. Pass a concrete instance as Params(; closure_level = ...): PresentClosures (the default) or PastClosures.

A closure that damps the state it is computed from (entrainment ∝ u★³/H dilutes U and thickens H; drag ∝ |u| slows u) is unstable in the leapfrog at the centred level once its rate exceeds about 0.57/Δt; from the lagged level it stays stable to well beyond that. LADDIE v1 lags the diffusion and the convective relaxation for the same reason. The density contrast δρ stays at the present level under both, since it also drives the pressure gradient.

source
Laddie.PresentClosures — Type
struct PresentClosures <: AbstractClosureLevel

Compute the closures from the present level (the default), as LADDIE v1 does.

source
Laddie.PastClosures — Type
struct PastClosures <: AbstractClosureLevel

Compute u★, the thickness in the entrainment rate, and the drag speed from the lagged, filtered past level. First order in time for these terms; combined with FluxMomentumForm it removes the step-to-step oscillation of thin, strongly entraining layers.

Select via Params(; closure_level = PastClosures()).
