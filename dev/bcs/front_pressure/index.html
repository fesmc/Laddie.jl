Ice-front pressure gradient

The pressure-gradient force in the momentum equations has three parts: the ice-base slope, the layer-thickness gradient and the density gradient (see the equation reference). The last two are the product-rule split of $-\nabla P$, with $P = \tfrac12 g\,\delta\rho\,H^2$ the depth-integrated reduced pressure of the layer, and difference a cell against its neighbour. At a one-sided face — the ice front, or a SinkGapsBC gap edge — that neighbour is not part of the active domain: what it holds is a condition on $P$ beyond the front, which Params(; front_pressure = ...) selects. With the front cell's $H$ and $\delta\rho$:

optionthickness / density termoutward tendency of $HU$ at the facecondition beyond the frontmatches
ZeroGradientFrontPressure (default)dropped / dropped0$\partial P/\partial n = 0$LADDIE v2 description paper
ZeroExteriorFrontPressuredropped / kept$\tfrac12 g\,\delta\rho\,H^2/\Delta x$$P = 0$ one cell outLADDIE v2 code
V1FrontPressurekept / kept$\tfrac32 g\,\delta\rho\,H^2/\Delta x$$P = 0$ one cell out, counted 3×LADDIE v1
  • ZeroExteriorFrontPressure is the one-sided difference of $P$ against $H = \delta\rho = 0$ in the neighbour, the values masking stores there. The LADDIE v2 code drops the thickness term at calving-front faces and keeps the density term, which gives the same force.
  • V1FrontPressure keeps both terms. The face averages of the split collapse to the front cell's values, so it sums to 3× the one-sided difference of $P$: the thickness term alone is ~150× its interior value on a warm ISOMIP+ run. It reproduces LADDIE v1, which Laddie.jl is ported from and validated against.
  • ZeroGradientFrontPressure drops both, as the zero lateral gradient at the calving front stated by Lambert et al. (2026). It is the default, with OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC; see Open-ocean boundary for the comparison.

Why zero gradient

Beyond the front, $P$ decays over some length $L$: a Robin condition $\partial P/\partial n = -P/L$, of which ZeroExteriorFrontPressure is $L = \Delta x$ and ZeroGradientFrontPressure is $L \to \infty$. Observed outflows leave the front as buoyant, geostrophic currents (Dotson) and keep rising beyond it (Pine Island), so $L$ is of the order of the Rossby radius (≈ 10 km), not $\Delta x$. At $\Delta x$ = 1–4 km zero gradient is the closer limit, and the only one whose front-row force does not grow as $1/\Delta x$ under refinement.

None of the options affects the grounding line, where no momentum equation is solved.

API

Laddie.AbstractFrontPressure — Type

Abstract supertype for the condition on the layer's reduced pressure P = ½ g δρ H² beyond one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC), which sets the layer-thickness- and density-gradient parts of the pressure-gradient force there. Pass a concrete instance as Params(; front_pressure = ...): ZeroGradientFrontPressure (the default), ZeroExteriorFrontPressure (LADDIE v2 code) or V1FrontPressure (LADDIE v1).

source
Laddie.ZeroGradientFrontPressure — Type
struct ZeroGradientFrontPressure <: AbstractFrontPressure

Zero normal gradient of P = ½ g δρ H² at one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC): drop both the layer-thickness- and density-gradient parts of the pressure-gradient force there, keeping the ice-base-slope part. The zero lateral gradient at the calving front of Lambert et al. (2026).

Choose it for runs at Δx below the Rossby radius, over which the outflow's buoyancy decays beyond the front (Ice-front pressure gradient).

Select via Params(; front_pressure = ZeroGradientFrontPressure()) (the default).

source
Laddie.ZeroExteriorFrontPressure — Type
struct ZeroExteriorFrontPressure <: AbstractFrontPressure

P = ½ g δρ H² = 0 one cell beyond one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC): the outward tendency of HU is ½ g δρ H²/Δx. Drops the layer-thickness-gradient part of the pressure-gradient force there and keeps the density part against the masked δρ = 0, as the LADDIE v2 code does at calving-front faces.

Choose it to reproduce LADDIE v2.

Select via Params(; front_pressure = ZeroExteriorFrontPressure()).

source
Laddie.V1FrontPressure — Type
struct V1FrontPressure <: AbstractFrontPressure

LADDIE v1's one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC): keep both the layer-thickness- and density-gradient parts of the pressure-gradient force against the masked neighbour's H = δρ = 0. Their face averages collapse to the front cell's values, so the outward tendency of HU is 3/2 g δρ H²/Δx, 3× that of ZeroExteriorFrontPressure.

Choose it to reproduce LADDIE v1; pinned in the v1 reproduction aliases.

Select via Params(; front_pressure = V1FrontPressure()).
