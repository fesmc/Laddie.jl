Wall slip

Grounding lines (mask value 2) and land (mask value 1: exposed bedrock, islands and the outer border ring) are zero-flux walls. The condition on the tangential momentum at each wall is one slip factor $s$ on the faces it bounds, with the convention of NEMO (rn_shlat): the velocity behind the wall is taken as a ghost $(1 - s)\,u$. The two kinds of wall are set independently, with BoundaryConditions(; grounding_line_slip = ..., land_slip = ...):

free slip, $s = 0$partial slipno slip, $s = 2$
grounding line, AbstractSlipAtGLFreeSlipAtGLPartialSlipAtGL(s), default s = 1NoSlipAtGL
land, AbstractSlipAtLandFreeSlipAtLandPartialSlipAtLand(s), default s = 1NoSlipAtLand

What $s$ means

Take a $u$-point next to a wall, with the wall parallel to $u$. On the C-grid the wall is the face between two rows of T-cells, half a grid spacing $\Delta$ from the $u$-point. The $u$-point mirrored across the wall, a full $\Delta$ away, lies behind the wall and carries no water. The stencils still read a velocity there, the ghost $u_g$, and the wall condition is nothing but the choice of $u_g$. With the velocity varying linearly between the two points, the velocity at the wall is their mean, $u_w = (u + u_g)/2$. Two physical limits fix $u_g$:

  • No slip: the water sticks to the wall, $u_w = 0$. The ghost must mirror the velocity with the opposite sign, $u_g = -u$.
  • Free slip: the wall exerts no stress, so the velocity has no gradient across it, $u_g = u$ and $u_w = u$.

Writing the ghost as $u_g = (1 - s)\,u$ puts free slip at $s = 0$ and no slip at $s = 2$, and gives

\[u_w = \left(1 - \frac{s}{2}\right) u :\]

$s/2$ is the fraction of the velocity the wall takes away. Halfway between the limits, $s = 1$ makes the ghost zero and keeps half the velocity at the wall: partial slip. It is also what a wall becomes when it is treated as an ordinary cell holding zero velocity, which is why it is LADDIE v2's wall and the default.

The tangential velocity $u$ next to the wall (blue), its ghost $u_g$ behind the wall (orange) and the linear profile through them (dashed). The dot marks the velocity $u_w$ on the wall face.

The viscous flux through the wall face follows from the same ghost:

\[A\,H\,\frac{u_g - u}{\Delta^2} = -s\,A\,H\,\frac{u}{\Delta^2},\]

so the wall stress grows linearly with $s$. Equivalently, the profile reaches zero at $\Delta/s$ from the $u$-point: never for free slip, at the ghost point for $s = 1$ and on the wall face for no slip.

The kernels form it from the velocity stored behind the wall, which is zero, so the plain stencil already holds $s = 1$; they add the remaining $s - 1$. Under NonlinearLateralViscosity the ghost enters the shear-scaled coefficient as well, so its wall stress grows as $s^2$.

The same ghost forms the momentum carried across a wall face by the advection, unless wall momentum advection says otherwise.

At a coastline corner whose stencil touches both grounded ice and exposed rock, the grounding-line condition takes precedence, so each wall face gets exactly one slip factor.

LADDIE v1 and v2

LADDIE v2 treats grounded and ice-free land neighbours as ordinary cells with zero velocity, one grid spacing away (laddie_velocity.f90:259-267), and carries no momentum across them. This is the default, $s = 1$ with NoWallAdvection. v2 calls it "no slip" (Lambert et al., 2026); in this convention it is partial slip.

LADDIE v1 describes its walls as NEMO-like partial slip with a factor between 0 (free slip) and 2 (no slip), and its published runs use 1 (Lambert et al., 2023). Its code, however, adds the factor to a viscous stencil that already holds the stored zero, so its viscous wall is one step stronger than labelled: $s = 1$ is no slip there. In the advection the factor is used as labelled. Reproducing v1 therefore takes V1WallAdvection:

# LADDIE v1 (slip = 1): no slip in the viscosity, partial slip in the advection
BoundaryConditions(; grounding_line_slip = NoSlipAtGL(), land_slip = NoSlipAtLand(),
                   wall_advection = V1WallAdvection())

# LADDIE v2: the defaults
BoundaryConditions()

API

Laddie.AbstractSlipAtGL — Type

Abstract supertype for the momentum boundary condition at grounding-line walls (mask value 2). Pass a concrete instance as BoundaryConditions(; grounding_line_slip = ...): PartialSlipAtGL (the default, PartialSlipAtGL(1.0), LADDIE v2's wall), NoSlipAtGL or FreeSlipAtGL.

Walls bordering exposed rock are governed separately by AbstractSlipAtLand.

Laddie.NoSlipAtGL — Type
struct NoSlipAtGL <: AbstractSlipAtGL

No-slip momentum boundary condition at the grounding line: slip factor 2 on walls bordering grounded ice (mask value 2), so the tangential velocity vanishes at the wall face. LADDIE v1's viscous wall; stronger than LADDIE v2's "no slip", which is PartialSlipAtGL(1.0). See Wall slip for the convention.

Select via BoundaryConditions(; grounding_line_slip = NoSlipAtGL()).

source
Laddie.FreeSlipAtGL — Type
struct FreeSlipAtGL <: AbstractSlipAtGL

Free-slip momentum boundary condition at the grounding line: slip factor 0 at walls bordering grounded ice, so the wall exerts no viscous stress on the flow.

Select via BoundaryConditions(; grounding_line_slip = FreeSlipAtGL()).

source
Laddie.PartialSlipAtGL — Type
struct PartialSlipAtGL{FT} <: AbstractSlipAtGL

Partial-slip momentum boundary condition at the grounding line, with a slip factor between 0 (free slip) and 2 (no slip): the velocity behind the wall is taken as (1 - factor)·u.

PartialSlipAtGL(1.0) is the default and LADDIE v2's wall: v2 treats a grounded neighbour as an ordinary cell with zero velocity, one grid spacing away (laddie_velocity.f90:259-267), which v2 calls "no slip" (Lambert et al. (2026)).

Select via BoundaryConditions(; grounding_line_slip = PartialSlipAtGL(1.0)) (the default).

Fields

  • factor::Any: slip factor, from 0 (free slip) to 2 (no slip)
source
Laddie.AbstractSlipAtLand — Type

Abstract supertype for the momentum boundary condition at land walls (mask value 1: exposed bedrock, islands, and the outer border ring). Pass a concrete instance as BoundaryConditions(; land_slip = ...): PartialSlipAtLand (the default, PartialSlipAtLand(1.0), LADDIE v2's wall), NoSlipAtLand or FreeSlipAtLand.

The grounding line is governed separately by AbstractSlipAtGL; at a coastline corner whose stencil touches both grounded ice and exposed rock, the grounding-line condition takes precedence (the land wall indicators exclude faces already flagged as grounding line), so each wall face gets exactly one slip factor.

source
Laddie.NoSlipAtLand — Type
struct NoSlipAtLand <: AbstractSlipAtLand

No-slip momentum boundary condition at land walls: the tangential velocity vanishes at walls bordering exposed bedrock (mask value 1), which includes islands and ice-free coastline inside the domain as well as the outer border ring. Implemented as a slip factor of 2 on land faces. See NoSlipAtGL for its relation to LADDIE v1 and v2.

Select via BoundaryConditions(; land_slip = NoSlipAtLand()).

Laddie.PartialSlipAtLand — Type
struct PartialSlipAtLand{FT} <: AbstractSlipAtLand

Partial-slip momentum boundary condition at land walls, with a slip factor between 0 (free slip) and 2 (no slip). PartialSlipAtLand(1.0) is the default and LADDIE v2's wall, where ice-free land cells, like grounded ones, hold zero velocity; see PartialSlipAtGL.
