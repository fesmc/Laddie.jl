Laplacian weights

All lateral Laplacians — tracer diffusion and momentum viscosity — are weighted by the layer thickness $H$. The diffused field itself is always taken at the lagged level past, as leapfrog stability requires (see Time stepping). Which time level of $H$ multiplies it is a choice, Params(; laplacian_weights = ...):

  • PresentLaplacianWeights, the default: H.present.
  • PastLaplacianWeights: the lagged, filtered H.past, so the weight sits on the same time level as the diffused field. This is what Python LADDIE v1.x does.

The difference is small. On the Crosson–Dotson validation case the two options give the same mean melt to 0.01 % and differ locally by at most 1.4 m in $H$.

API

Laddie.AbstractLaplacianWeights — Type

Abstract supertype for the time level of the layer thickness H that weights all lateral Laplacians (tracer diffusion and momentum viscosity). Pass a concrete instance as Params(; laplacian_weights = ...): PresentLaplacianWeights (the default) or PastLaplacianWeights.

The diffused field itself is always taken at the lagged level past, as leapfrog stability requires; this choice only concerns the thickness that multiplies it. On the Crosson–Dotson validation case the two options give the same mean melt to 0.01 % and differ locally by at most 1.4 m in H.
