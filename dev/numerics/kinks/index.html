Kinks

The model is piecewise smooth. Floors and caps (max, min, clamp), the upwind choice of the donor side, the speed cap and √ at zero all have a derivative that jumps, or grows without bound, at one point. The derivative is exact everywhere else, so a gradient from automatic differentiation is correct almost everywhere. Still, gradient-based optimisers can stall on a kink, and over a long window an adjoint that multiplies through such switches every step can grow much faster than the finite-perturbation response does.

Params(; kinks = ...) chooses how they are treated:

  • ExactKinks, the default, keeps every kink. The model is bit-identical to one without a kink treatment.

  • SmoothKinks replaces each kink by a $C^1$ blend over a band of half-width ε, with one ε per physical quantity, and is exact outside the band. max(x, 0) becomes

    \[\max{}_\varepsilon(x) = \begin{cases} 0, & x \le -\varepsilon \\ (x + \varepsilon)^2 / 4\varepsilon, & |x| < \varepsilon \\ x, & x \ge \varepsilon, \end{cases}\]

    and max(a, b), min(a, b), clamp, |u| and the upwind split $u^\pm$ follow from it ($u^+ + u^- = u$ holds exactly, so the upwind fluxes stay conservative). √max(x, 0) becomes the quadratic $(x + 3\varepsilon^2)^2 / 16\varepsilon^3$ below $x = \varepsilon^2$, whose slope stays below $1/2\varepsilon$. The step of the conductive $c_i$ at the freezing point is blended with a cubic smoothstep.

An ε of zero keeps that group exact, so the groups can be switched on one at a time to find the ones that matter:

Params(; kinks = SmoothKinks())                                           # all on
Params(; kinks = SmoothKinks(; H = 0.0, u = 0.0, uH = 0.0, w = 0.0,
                               drho = 0.0, T = 0.0, S = 0.0, log = 0.1))   # γ_T only

Kinks that the state sits on exactly, rather than crosses, are left exact. A blend there would shift the result by about ε/4 in every step. This covers the max(0, ⋅) guard of the cap detrainment, the clamp of H after the thickness step and the share of the flux-form tracer update that follows it: the minimum-thickness entrainment and the cap detrainment bring the stepped H exactly to its bounds.

On a 3-day ISOMIP+ run with the LADDIE v2 schemes, the default widths move the mean melt by less than 0.1 %. In a cold setup where the T_max bound binds, the T group moves it by about 1 %.

API

Laddie.AbstractKinks — Type
abstract type AbstractKinks

Abstract supertype for how the model treats its kinks: the switches of max, min, clamp and upwinding, and the unbounded slope of √x at zero. Pass a concrete instance as Params(; kinks = ...): ExactKinks (the default) or SmoothKinks.

The kinks make the model piecewise smooth. A derivative exists almost everywhere, but it jumps where a switch flips, and gradient-based optimisers can stall there. Over a long window, an adjoint that multiplies through such switches every step can also grow much faster than the finite-difference response does. SmoothKinks replaces each switch by a $C^1$ blend over a band of width ε, outside which the model is unchanged.

Laddie.ExactKinks — Type
struct ExactKinks <: AbstractKinks

Keep every max, min, clamp, upwind switch and √ as it is (the default). The model is then bit-identical to one without a kink treatment.

Select via Params(; kinks = ExactKinks()) (the default).

Laddie.SmoothKinks — Type
struct SmoothKinks{FT} <: AbstractKinks

Smooth every kink over a band of half-width ε, one ε per physical quantity:

  • max(x, 0) becomes the $C^1$ quadratic blend $(x + ε)^2 / 4ε$ for $|x| < ε$ and is exact outside the band. max(a, b), min(a, b), clamp, |x| and the upwind splits $u^\pm$ follow from it, so $u^+ + u^- = u$ still holds and the upwind fluxes stay conservative.
  • √x (of max(x, 0)) is blended into the quadratic $(x + 3ε^2)^2 / 16ε^3$ below $x = ε^2$, which removes the unbounded slope at zero, and is exact above it.
  • The step of the conductive c_i at the freezing point is blended with a cubic smoothstep over $T - T_f \in [-ε_T, ε_T]$.

Where each ε acts:

εwhere
HH_min top-up, cap detrainment against the cap and the H_min floor
uupwind tracer and thickness fluxes, the centred momentum advection's sign(u), the speed cap, √ of the drag, speed, deformation and Holland's shear
uHdonor of the momentum under UpstreamMomentumAdvection (sign of the face mass flux)
wsplit of the Gaspar rate into entrainment and detrainment, max_detrainment, the melt discriminant
drhodrho_floor of Gaspar entrainment, the ClampDensity floor
Tconductive c_i at the freezing point, frazil supercooling, T_min/T_max
SS_min/S_max
logfloor of the log-layer term of TurbulentGamTMelting

An ε of zero keeps that group's kinks exact, so the groups can be switched on one at a time.

Not smoothed are the kinks that the state sits on exactly rather than crosses, where a blend would bias every step by about ε/4: the outer max(0, ⋅) of the cap detrainment (zero wherever the cap does not bind), the clamp of H after the thickness step (a safety net behind ent2 and the cap detrainment, which bring the stepped H exactly to its bounds) and the share of the flux-form tracer update that follows that clamp. Nor are the convective switches of ResetToAmbient and RelaxToAmbient, the γ_T of PrescribedMelting, the depth interpolation of the forcing, or the time-step controller.

Select via Params(; kinks = SmoothKinks()), or e.g. SmoothKinks(; H = 0.5).

Fields

  • H::Any: layer thickness (m, default 0.1)

  • u::Any: velocity (m s⁻¹, default 1e-3)

  • uH::Any: face mass flux H·u (m² s⁻¹, default 1e-3)

  • w::Any: entrainment, detrainment and melt rate (m s⁻¹, default 1e-8, about 0.3 m yr⁻¹)

  • drho::Any: reduced density contrast δρ (–, default 1e-6)

  • T::Any: temperature (K, default 0.01)

  • S::Any: salinity (psu, default 0.01)

  • log::Any: log-layer term log(u★H/ν₀) (–, default 0.1)
