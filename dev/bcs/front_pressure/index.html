Ice-front pressure gradient

The pressure-gradient force in the momentum equations has three parts: the ice-base slope, the layer-thickness gradient and the density gradient (see the equation reference). The last two difference a cell against its neighbour. At a one-sided face — the ice front, or a SinkGapsBC gap edge — that neighbour is not part of the active domain, and masking pins its stored $H$ and $\delta\rho$ to 0. The thickness-gradient term $g\,\overline{H\delta\rho}\,(H_{x-1}-H)/\Delta x$ is then a full one-sided gradient, as if $H$ collapsed to zero across one grid cell.

LADDIE v1 and v2 disagree on what to do about this, so Params(; front_pressure = ...) makes it a choice:

optionthickness gradientdensity gradientmatches
FullDepthFrontPressurekeptkeptLADDIE v1
TruncatedDepthFrontPressuredroppedkeptLADDIE v2
ZeroGradientFrontPressure (default)droppeddroppedLADDIE v2 description paper
  • FullDepthFrontPressure reproduces LADDIE v1, which Laddie.jl is ported from and validated against. Measured on a warm ISOMIP+ run, the thickness-gradient term is ~150× larger at the ice front than in the interior under it.
  • TruncatedDepthFrontPressure drops the thickness-gradient term, as the LADDIE v2 code does at calving-front faces.
  • ZeroGradientFrontPressure also drops the density-gradient term $\tfrac12 g\,\bar H^2\,(\delta\rho_{x-1}-\delta\rho)/\Delta x$. This is the zero lateral gradient at the calving front stated in the LADDIE v2 description paper (Lambert et al. (2026)); the v2 code keeps the density term. It is the default, with OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC; see Open-ocean boundary for the comparison.

None of them affects the grounding line, where no momentum equation is solved.

API

Laddie.AbstractFrontPressure — Type

Abstract supertype for the layer-thickness- and density-gradient parts of the pressure-gradient force at one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC). Pass a concrete instance as Params(; front_pressure = ...): ZeroGradientFrontPressure (the default), FullDepthFrontPressure (LADDIE v1) or TruncatedDepthFrontPressure (LADDIE v2).

source
Laddie.FullDepthFrontPressure — Type
struct FullDepthFrontPressure <: AbstractFrontPressure

Keep the layer-thickness-gradient part of the pressure-gradient force at one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC), evaluated as (H_neighbour - H)/Δ with the neighbour's masked H = 0: a full one-sided gradient. LADDIE v1's choice, pinned in the v1 reproduction aliases.

Select via Params(; front_pressure = FullDepthFrontPressure()).

source
Laddie.TruncatedDepthFrontPressure — Type
struct TruncatedDepthFrontPressure <: AbstractFrontPressure

Drop the layer-thickness-gradient part of the pressure-gradient force at one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC), keeping the ice-base-slope and density-gradient parts. LADDIE v2's code at calving-front faces.

Select via Params(; front_pressure = TruncatedDepthFrontPressure()).

source
Laddie.ZeroGradientFrontPressure — Type
struct ZeroGradientFrontPressure <: AbstractFrontPressure

Drop both the layer-thickness-gradient and the density-gradient parts of the pressure-gradient force at one-sided faces (the ice front, and gap edges under SinkGapsBC), keeping only the ice-base-slope part: the zero lateral gradient at the calving front stated by Lambert et al. (2026).

The default, with OutflowOnlyOpenOceanBC; the options are compared on the Ice-front pressure gradient page.

Select via Params(; front_pressure = ZeroGradientFrontPressure()) (the default).
