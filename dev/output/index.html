Output and restarts

A Simulation writes its files as its OutputConfig says, into joinpath(resultdir, name). A plain OutputConfig() writes nothing; set saveday > 0 to write

  • time-averaged maps in output.nc, every saveday days;
  • a line of diagnostics in log.txt, every diagday days;
  • a JLD2 restart every restday days, and one at the end of every run!.

Event times are absolute on the simulation clock, so a simulation advanced by several run! calls writes on the same cadence as one advanced by a single call.

Maps

OutputConfig(; fields) names the variables of output.nc. Time averages over each saveday window:

namefieldunits
:Ut, :Vtx- and y-velocity averaged onto T-pointsm s⁻¹
:Uu, :Vvx- and y-velocity on their own u- and v-pointsm s⁻¹
:H, :T, :Slayer thickness, temperature, salinitym, °C, psu
:meltbasal melt ratem yr⁻¹
:entr, :detrentrainment and detrainment ratesm yr⁻¹
:ent2the extra entrainment that keeps H ≥ H_minm yr⁻¹
:detr_capthe detrainment that keeps H under max_layer_thicknessm yr⁻¹
:frazilfrazil-ice formation, freshwater equivalent (zero unless RelaxedFrazilIce)m yr⁻¹
:Tbase, :Tambtemperature at the ice–ocean interface, ambient temperature at the layer base°C
:gammaTturbulent heat-exchange velocitym s⁻¹
:ustarfriction velocity at the ice basem s⁻¹
:drhoreduced density contrast with the ambient water–
:convective_instabilityfraction of time a cell was convectively unstable–
:supercooling$T - T_f(S, z_b)$, the layer temperature above its freezing point at the ice base (negative where supercooled)°C

Static fields, written once: :mask (the mask and the margin flags at_isf, at_grl, at_lnd, at_gap) and :z_draft (the ice draft, m).

The maps are written as float_type (Float32 by default, Float64 on request), with the fill value NaN on every cell outside the active cells (tmask), and compressed losslessly (deflate level 1 with shuffle). NetCDF readers return these cells as missing (NCDatasets) or NaN (xarray, ncview).

Time series

Off by default. With tsday set, every tsday days output.nc gets the means over the ice-covered cells (imask) of the fields averaged over that window, on their own time axis ts_time:

nameseriesunits
ts_H, ts_T, ts_Smean layer thickness, temperature, salinitym, °C, psu
ts_tfmean thermal forcing $T - T_f(S, z_b)$ (the mean of :supercooling)°C
ts_u_normmean speed on T-pointsm s⁻¹
ts_mean_meltmean net basal melt rate, melt − frazilm yr⁻¹
ts_total_melttotal net basal melt, freshwater massGt yr⁻¹
ts_refreezingtotal refreezing, max(−melt, 0) + frazil, freshwater massGt yr⁻¹

As in meltstats, ts_total_melt + ts_refreezing is the melting alone. The fields are accumulated on the device every step, like the maps; the spatial means are reduced only once per tsday. Not available under a ReactantBackend: to_backend rejects a simulation with tsday set.

Melt statistics

meltstats reduces the current state to max_melt, mean_melt, max_speed, total_melt and refreezing, on the model's device (on a CPU copy under the Reactant backend).

  • The net basal melt is the interface melt rate melt (negative where the base refreezes) minus the frazil formation frazil: the basal mass balance of the ice. refreezing is the ice formed, $\sum (\max(-\dot m, 0) + \dot f)$, so total_melt + refreezing is the melting alone.
  • Rates are freshwater equivalent, the unit the model integrates; multiply by rho_freshwater / rho_ice for ice equivalent. The mass fluxes are $\sum \text{rate}\,\Delta x\,\Delta y\,\rho_\text{fw}$, the same in either unit.
  • The melt statistics average over the ice-covered cells (imask), not the active cells (tmask). Under ConnectedGapsBC gap cells are active but ice-free and melt exactly zero, so tmask would dilute the mean by the gap area. LADDIE v2 integrates over its domain_a, gaps included; without gaps the two coincide. max_speed stays on tmask, since the layer does flow through gaps.

Restarts

A restart file holds the three leapfrog levels of H, U, V, T and S, the time, the step that separates the levels and the active cells, as CPU arrays at the model's precision; restart_latest.jld2 is always the newest. init_from_restart!, or the restart keyword of Simulation, loads it onto any backend and precision.

Continuation. On the same grid a restart continues the run exactly: a run of 2 days equals one of 1 day, restarted, plus 1 day, to the last bit. This needs the same dt. AdaptiveTimeStepper resumes at the file's dt. FixedTimeStepper, and a stepper of your own, keeps the dt the simulation was built with; when it differs from the file's, the leapfrog restarts with one forward step at the new dt, and the log says so. A file written before the dt was stored restarts the leapfrog the same way. A file that stores the active cells must match the model's.

Refinement. With refine = f > 1, the file is the restart of a run whose cells are f times coarser on the same full domain:

  • each coarse cell holds f × f fine ones; the fine domain, border ring included, is f .* (coarse size .- 2) .+ 2 cells, or up to f - 1 more at the far end of an axis (a grid coarsened by whole blocks drops the remainder of its source grid), and those extra cells keep the state the simulation was built with;
  • each fine cell takes H, T and S of the coarse cell it lies in, where that cell was active, and each fine velocity point the cell-centred velocity of that coarse cell (the mean of its two faces), on the fine velocity masks;
  • fine cells whose coarse cell was not active (a finer shelf reaches further) keep their state, so refine onto a simulation started from rest;
  • dt is the restart's divided by f, and the leapfrog restarts with a forward step;
  • the two runs may crop their domains differently; the coarse grid is read from the file.
