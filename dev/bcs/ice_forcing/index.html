Ice forcing

The ice forcing supplies the far-field ice temperature T_ice_farfield: the temperature of the ice above the basal boundary layer, typically the surface temperature. It enters the heat conducted into the ice at the base, through the ice-conduction scheme, and so the melting parameterisations.

T_ice_farfield is the temperature of the ice away from the base, not at it. The base is at the interface temperature $T_b$, the freezing point, which the melt solve computes every step; were $T_i$ the basal temperature, $T_i = T_b$ and it would have no effect. The ice column is not resolved: the heat conducted into the ice is parameterised, and $T_i$ is the temperature above the thin thermal boundary layer at the base, into which the ice is advected as it melts.

  • For a steady ice column, as assumed by HollandJenkinsIceConduction (Holland & Jenkins 1999) and the default MeltOnlyIceConduction, it is the ice-shelf surface temperature; Wiskandt & Jourdain (2025) use the 1980–2019 mean snow-surface temperature.
  • With an ice-sheet model at hand, the ice temperature 50–100 m above the base, as a 2D field, carries the transient and upstream-advected state of the ice instead.

Colder ice absorbs more heat per unit melt, so a lower T_ice_farfield gives less melt for the same thermal driving: the effective latent heat runs from 3.34e5 J kg⁻¹ at 0 °C to 3.84e5 J kg⁻¹ at −25 °C, a 15 % spread.

T_ice_farfield is a scalar (uniform ice) or a matrix the size of mask, letting a colder interior sit next to a temperate ice front; Model converts either to a field on the grid, cropped with the mask.

A model takes it as the second half of its CavityForcing. Passing an ocean forcing on its own to Model pairs it with a uniform PrescribedIceForcing(-25.0).

API

Laddie.AbstractIceForcing — Type

Supertype of every ice forcing: the state of the ice above the layer. Subtypes provide a field T_ice_farfield (°C, ≤ 0), read by the AbstractIceConduction scheme.

source
Laddie.PrescribedIceForcing — Type
struct PrescribedIceForcing{M} <: AbstractIceForcing

Ice temperature T_ice_farfield (°C) held fixed for the whole run: the temperature of the ice above the basal boundary layer, typically the surface temperature, not the basal temperature. A scalar, or a matrix the size of mask that Model crops with the grid. Its meaning and range are on the Ice forcing page.

julia> PrescribedIceForcing(-25.0)   # uniform, the default
PrescribedIceForcing(T_ice_farfield = -25.0 °C)

Fields

  • T_ice_farfield::Any: ice temperature above the basal boundary layer, typically the surface temperature (°C): a scalar, or a full-domain matrix the size of mask
